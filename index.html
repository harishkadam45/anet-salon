Meet Anet

Beauty that looks like you walked in — because you did.

I'm Anet — a lash and brow artist working out of a private, by-appointment studio in Sherman Oaks. I started doing lashes because I was tired of sets that looked great for one week and then fell apart by the next.

My approach is simple: I map every set to your eye shape, your natural lash strength and how much time you actually want to spend on your mornings. Nothing heavy, nothing overdone — just lashes that still look good on day ten.

I take my time with every client, and I never rush an appointment. That's why my books fill quickly.

  • One artist, start to finish. No hand-offs — you work with me at every visit, so I learn your lashes and keep them consistent.
  • Tailored mapping, not a package. We'll go over your desired look, your natural lashes and your aftercare before we start.
  • Sanitary, private studio. Fresh or single-use implements, vented workstation, and a calm room you won't be sharing.
  • Honest aftercare guidance. I'll tell you exactly how long your set should last and how to get the most out of it.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Do I need a patch test before getting lash extensions?
Not for most people, but if you've had a reaction to adhesives or tape before, tell me before we start. We use a latex-free, medical-grade adhesive and I'll do a small patch if you'd like the peace of mind. Unpatched lashes mean you're ready for a full set right away.
How long do lash extensions actually last?
Your natural lashes grow about 0.1mm per day, so a full set typically lasts 4–6 weeks while it grows out. Most clients book a refill every 2–3 weeks to keep the set looking brand new. I'll give you an aftercare guide so your retention is as good as possible.
Do I need a full removal before booking a new set?
Yes — if you're coming from another artist and still have lashes on, please book a removal first. It protects your natural lashes and usually takes 20–30 minutes on its own. It's a quick add-on and I'll always tell you honestly if a removal is needed.
Can I get lashes and brows in the same appointment?
Absolutely — that's what our combos are for, and you'll save compared with booking separately. Book the lash set + brow shape combo, or the full refresh (lash lift + brow lamination) if you'd rather skip extensions entirely.
Where exactly are you located?
We're a private, by-appointment studio in Sherman Oaks. Full address and directions are on the Yelp listing — parking is available in the building.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept cash, card (Apple Pay / Google Pay), Zelle and Venmo. A card is the easiest since it holds your appointment slot, but any method works.
What is your cancellation policy?
Please give at least 24 hours notice to reschedule or cancel. Late cancellations and no-shows are charged 50% of the service total, since that time is reserved exclusively for you.
I'm nervous about getting my first set. Is that normal?
Completely normal, and the most common thing I hear. There's no pressure to close your eyes — many clients like to keep them open and talk. I work at a pace you set, and I'll talk you through exactly what I'm doing the whole time.
